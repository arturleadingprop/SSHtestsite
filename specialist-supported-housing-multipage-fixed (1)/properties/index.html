About the opportunity

Homes that serve a purpose. Investments that deserve a closer look.

Specialist Supported Housing is a form of residential accommodation designed for people who need additional support to live as independently as possible. Properties are typically managed through specialist housing or care providers under agreements that set out the responsibilities of each party.
